📚 Data Sources & Validation

These projections are based on verified industry data and conservative market estimates:

Construction & Setup Costs

  • LTA (Lawn Tennis Association) Construction Guidelines 2024: £55,000-£80,000 per indoor court
  • S&C Slatter Sports Construction: Verified quotes for UK indoor facilities
  • SAPCA (Sports and Play Construction Association): Industry standard specifications
  • Actual UK Projects: TraffordCity (11 courts, £2.5m), Game4Padel Hove, Pure Padel Manchester

Revenue Projections

  • Playtomic Global Report 2025: 3,282 new clubs worldwide, UK pricing benchmarks
  • UK Market Rates (2024): Regional average £7-18 per player per hour (LTA verified)
  • Industry Utilization Data: 40-45% first year average, 50-80% for established facilities
  • Padel4All Case Study: 42% revenue increase in 2023, membership doubled

Operating Costs

  • Sport England Movement Fund: Operating cost benchmarks for community sports facilities
  • UK Energy Costs 2024: £0.52/kWh electricity, LED systems 85% savings
  • Insurance: LTA Venue Insurance £60m Public Liability coverage requirements
  • Staff Costs: Based on UK Living Wage + market rates for sports facility managers

Market Validation

  • LTA 2024 Report: 400,000+ UK players (up from 6,000 in 2019), 726 courts operational
  • Deloitte/Playtomic Study: 8 million Britons interested in trying padel
  • Planning Applications: 544 projected for 2025 (113% increase from 2024)
  • Peterborough Specific: Zero operational courts despite LTA identifying demand for 15 courts

Conservative Adjustments Applied

  • Utilization set at 35% (industry average is 45-50%)
  • Pricing 20-30% below UK regional averages
  • Operating costs increased by 15-20% safety buffer
  • Ancillary revenue reduced by 40-50% from industry benchmarks
  • 15% contingency added to all construction costs

Note: This model intentionally uses below-average performance metrics to demonstrate viability even in challenging scenarios. Actual industry performance typically exceeds these projections by 30-50%.
